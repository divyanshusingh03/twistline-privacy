Privacy Policy

Twistline: Arrow Puzzle · Effective date: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Twistline: Arrow Puzzle (the “App”), published by DS Apps Lab (“we”, “us”). It explains what information the App handles when you play it.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. The App does not ask you to create an account, sign in, or provide your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal data.

To let you continue playing, the App saves your game progress (such as your current level, remaining hints, and sound and vibration settings) locally on your device. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us. You can remove it at any time by clearing the App’s data or uninstalling the App.

3. Third-party services

The current version of the App does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking services, and it does not share any data with third parties. If we add such features in a future version (for example, advertising or in-app purchases), we will update this policy to describe them before that version is released.

4. Children’s privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the App does not collect personal information, there is no personal information from children to delete or share.

5. Security

Since the App does not collect or transmit personal information, there is no personal data held by us that could be exposed. Game progress stored on your device is protected by your device’s own security features.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
